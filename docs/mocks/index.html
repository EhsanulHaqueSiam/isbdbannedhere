Data model

Entity      university | country | program    name, aliases, domains, country
 └ Event    paused | restricted | resumed | extended | denied
            rows[], from, until?, evidence: official | press | community
    └ Source   normalized URL, publisher, date, quote, archive URL, story fingerprint
Submission  URL, extracted claims[], decision per claim, pending | approved | merged | rejected

Status per row comes from the latest approved event covering it, never edited by hand. One article can feed many events: the FT story behind the UK pauses named ten universities. One event can have many sources.

Duplicate handling

LayerHowOutcome
1 · Same linkNormalize: https, lowercase host, drop www., m., AMP, utm_*, fbclid, fragments, trailing slash. Follow redirects, prefer rel=canonical. Unique index.Blocked. Shows where it already lives.
2 · Same storyTitle and lead fingerprint against the last 30 days of sources. The FT story ran in the Daily Star, Gulf News, The News, Aaj, ANN and Bonik Barta.Extra source on the existing events. More outlets, more confidence. Never new events.
3 · Same institutionPick from a canonical list (UKVI sponsor register, world university domains list) with aliases and email domains. No free text."Univ. of Herts" and "UH" land on one page. Missing ones go through a request.
4 · Same eventSame entity, same action, overlapping rows, dates within 60 days."Already tracked, add as source". A different action is offered as a status change.
5 · MergeReviewer merges events or entities that slipped through.Sources move, old links redirect, aliases stay.

Status rules

  • No source, no status change. A change without a link is saved as a tip for reviewers.
  • An end date passing makes a pause Needs re-check, not Resumed. We don't know until someone finds a source.
  • Evidence tiers: Official (university or government page) beats Press beats Community. Community-only shows as Unconfirmed.
  • If the university denies a report, the event is kept with both sources and the row shows Disputed.
  • Sign in with Google to submit. Reading is open.
