D in words. Status in a full sentence that names who and what. One paragraph that a visitor can repeat to a friend. One date line. The newest source as a card. Three share buttons. The month strip, "28 / 15 / 1/4" tiles, the eight-button grid, the card picker and the sticky bottom bar all move below the reports or go.

Not in these mocks

  • Which "companies" belong on the site at all. Apple Pay and Amazon Pay never launched in Bangladesh; shown next to visa bans with the same red icon they make the site look wrong. Needs your call.
  • Bangla copy. Same layout, rewritten sentences, not literal translations of "places we track".
  • The lag (1.3 MB of JS on the home page). Separate, mechanical fix.
