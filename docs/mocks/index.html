Adding a news link, for every category

  1. Paste a link. The server fetches it, reads the title, outlet, date and preview image, and saves an archive copy.
  2. It pulls out every claim, across categories: one article can name a country, a city and a company. Each claim gets a flag or logo so it's easy to check.
  3. Duplicate check per claim: new, already tracked (adds a receipt), or conflicting (becomes Disputed with both sides shown).
  4. The article shows up as a credited preview on every page it supports, with the outlet and date.
