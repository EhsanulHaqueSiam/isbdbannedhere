Why v6 felt like good news

  • Cream paper and an elegant italic serif is the look of magazines and wedding invites. Calm, aspirational.
  • The flag's red disc and green are what Bangladeshis post on Victory Day. They read as pride, not alarm.
  • Soft rounded cards, grain, an emoji told the eye "relax".
  • Gentle verbs ("isn't taking") instead of what happened (shut out, refused, blocked).

Pick a tone: Alert, Red, Struck off, or a mix. My lean: Alert for the app and most cards (urgent but credible, reads like news), Struck off as the white theme and for the personal shortlist card (crossed-out names are the most personal image here), Red saved for campaign days when we want maximum noise.
