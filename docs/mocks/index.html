isbdbannedhere

Does it work in Bangladesh? One place to check whether a service works from Bangladesh, what exactly breaks (opening it, signing up, paying, getting paid), who blocks it (the service or the BD government), and what to use instead.

Verdict model

Every service has four rows. The verdict is derived from them, never typed by hand. Every row carries a source and a checked date; rows older than 90 days show as unverified.

RowQuestion
OpenDoes the site or app load on BD ISPs without a VPN?
JoinCan a BD resident sign up and pass KYC (+880 OTP, NID or passport)?
PayDoes a BD-issued card work?
EarnCan you receive money or payouts to BD?
VerdictWhen
✓ WorksEvery applicable row passes.
◐ PartialSome rows fail. Example: you can pay for it, but you can't get paid.
✕ Not hereThe service excludes Bangladesh (licensing, sanctions risk, business choice).
⊘ Blocked in BDBTRC, a court order, or the government blocks it.
? GreyIt works, but using it carries legal risk under BD law (crypto).

Decisions

  • Verdict is computed from the rows, so a page can't say "Works" with a red row.
  • Legal, ToS-safe workarounds only. No VPN or fake-address guides: they get accounts banned, carry BD legal risk, and would get the site itself blocked.
  • Mobile first. Traffic will come from Facebook groups on phones.
  • Data lives in git until something needs per-user state. The backend arrives with reports, wants and alerts, not before.
  • Out of scope: travel and visa bans. Different audience, different data.
